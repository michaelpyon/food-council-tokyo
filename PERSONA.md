# PERSONA.md - Food Council: Tokyo

## The 1 person

**Hana Okada, 31, senior product designer at a Brooklyn fintech.** Lapsed Japanese minor, reads hiragana slowly, flying JFK to Haneda in 6 weeks for a 9-day trip with 2 friends. She is the designated "food person" of the group and she takes that job seriously.

Verdict on the V2 evangelist: **kept, not overruled.** The product is literally shaped around her job (trusted composite scores + a shareable saved-trip URL). Alternative candidates were considered and rejected: a Tokyo expat foodie already reads Tabelog natively and does not need this; a casual tourist does not care about methodology and will not share a data product. Hana is the person who both bounces hard on fake data AND evangelizes when it is real. This pass sharpens her rather than replaces her.

## The exact job she hires this for

Compress 2 months of untrusted listicle research into 1 trustworthy shortlist, then hand her group chat a single clean URL of her saved picks, grouped by neighborhood, each pre-linked to Tabelog and Google Maps. The deliverable is not "browsing restaurants." The deliverable is **the artifact she sends**: her trip list as a link that makes her look like she did the work (because she did).

## Her vocabulary

Says "Tabelog 3.7 is elite, 4.0 is god-tier." Says "omakase," "bib gourmand," "tourist trap," "the 38," "pins," "is this list load-bearing?" Distinguishes shoyu from shio without thinking. Allergic to the words "hidden gems await" and "foodie paradise."

## Device

iPhone 15 Pro, Safari, usually 1-handed on the subway. Desktop Chrome only during Friday-night planning sessions. The share moment happens on the phone; the research happens on desktop. Both must be first-class.

## 5-second bounce triggers

1. Generic hero copy ("Discover Tokyo's best restaurants") = instant back button
2. No recognizable anchor names above the fold (Saito, Nakiryu, Den, Jiro) = "this is AI-generated filler"
3. Hidden or absent scoring methodology = "another listicle wearing a UI"
4. Stock photos of generic ramen implying a specific restaurant = trust destroyed permanently
5. Saved list that dead-ends in localStorage with no URL out = "why did I do this work"

## The unprompted-share moment

She finishes saving 12 spots, taps share, and the link unfurls in "TOKYO 🍜" showing **her trip**: "Hana's Tokyo list - 12 spots, 4 Michelin stars, 5 neighborhoods." Grouped by area, every pick 1 tap from Tabelog and Maps. That unfurl is the screenshot. The public version is her finally posting the r/JapanTravel comment: "Here is my 9-day Tokyo food list, grouped by area, with Tabelog + Maps links for each: [link]" and watching it collect upvotes because the data checks out when strangers audit it. Strangers WILL audit it; that is the whole bet.
