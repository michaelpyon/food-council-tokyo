## Design Context

### Users

The primary user is Hana Okada, a 31-year-old Tokyo trip planner doing serious restaurant research for a small group. She researches on desktop, shares and revisits the list on an iPhone, and rejects generic travel copy, unverifiable ratings, misleading restaurant photos, and dead-end saved lists.

Her job is to turn scattered research into a trustworthy shortlist grouped by neighborhood, then send that exact trip to her group chat. Every saved restaurant needs an honest identity, current operating status, useful location context, and a link that lets her verify the place herself.

### Brand Personality

Exact, editorial, and skeptical. Food Council speaks like a reference desk with a point of view, not an influencer or delivery app. Trust comes from named sources, visible verification dates, precise counts, and plain disclosure when a field is unknown.

### Aesthetic Direction

Preserve the light editorial-paper system: warm off-white background, near-black ink, hairline borders, Playfair Display for restaurant and feature headlines, Inter for body and controls, and a single vermilion accent. Michelin gold is reserved for verified Michelin distinctions.

The interface should resemble a compact magazine index crossed with a trip-planning document. Avoid generic restaurant photography, stock food imagery, gradients, glass surfaces, app-store card grids, dark neon styling, urgency badges, and decorative data.

Reference products: Letterboxd for list identity, The Infatuation for editorial discipline, and Wanderlog for the shareable trip artifact.

### Design Principles

1. Evidence before decoration. Show only claims the product can source, and put verification links within 1 tap.
2. Unknown is a valid state. Remove unsupported ratings, prices, descriptions, and images instead of replacing them with estimates.
3. The trip list is the product. Browsing, filtering, saving, and sharing must preserve the same verified restaurant identities.
4. Mobile is a first-class planning surface. Controls, cards, panels, and share actions must work one-handed at 375 pixels wide.
5. Exact counts and dates carry trust. Any visible total must come from the current publishable dataset, and the verification date must stay visible.

### Current design posture

- Mode: preserve
- Design variance: 7
- Motion intensity: 4
- Visual density: 5
- Accessibility: WCAG AA, visible focus, keyboard-complete panels, reduced-motion support
- Canonical product data: a generated publishable dataset derived from the provenance audit, never the unaudited source file
